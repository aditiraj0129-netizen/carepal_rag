# Products

## The CarePal app

The app lets users book caregivers, track visits and manage the family's care from a phone, on iOS and Android. Features include scheduling trained nurses at home, real-time care updates, health and medicine reminders, caregiver visit and shift management, family care coordination, doctor consultation booking and access to medical records.

## Telmideed 40

A blood pressure medication described as a once-daily dosage offering up to 24-hour control of blood pressure levels. It is not suitable during pregnancy or for patients with renal or hepatic impairment, and the page advises consulting a professional before combining it with NSAIDs, lithium or potassium-sparing diuretics. It should be taken under medical guidance. The page does not explain how to purchase it and states no prices.
