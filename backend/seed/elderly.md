# Elderly Care

TheCarePal provides experienced caregivers who offer compassionate assistance and companionship for seniors at home, aiming for comfort and dignity.

## What elderly care includes

- Daily living support: help with meals, bathing, grooming and mobility in a respectful and gentle manner.
- Medication and medical support: timely medication reminders and coordination with nurses or doctors when needed.
- Memory and cognitive care: support for seniors with dementia or Alzheimer's through structured routines and patience.
- Family communication: regular updates shared with family members about the senior's health and care progress.
- Companionship and emotional well-being: friendly caregivers who ease loneliness.

## How to book

Sign up or click Book Elderly Care. TheCarePal matches a caregiver to the senior's needs.

### Are caregivers verified?

Caregivers are vetted and experienced in senior support before being matched with a family. The page does not describe the vetting process or any certification.

### Can elderly care be combined with dementia or Alzheimer's support?

Yes. Many caregivers are trained in memory care, and elderly care and dementia or Alzheimer's support can be provided together.
