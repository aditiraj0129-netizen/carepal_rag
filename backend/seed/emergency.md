# Emergency Care

Fast emergency care delivered at home by a professional. The team is described as available around the clock to provide immediate care and support.

## What the page says

Emergency care aims to provide quick response times for urgent situations, support until further medical help is available, and a bridge between routine care and urgent medical attention. The page does not give response times, service areas, prices or a list of conditions covered. It does not say that this service replaces an ambulance or hospital emergency care.

### How do I get emergency care?

Use the Get Emergency Care button in the app, Talk to an Expert, or Book an Appointment. In the CarePal app, one-tap calls are listed for the emergency numbers 112, 102, 101 and 108.
