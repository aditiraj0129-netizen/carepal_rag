# Dementia Care

Dementia refers to a group of conditions that affect memory, thinking and daily functioning, especially in older adults. TheCarePal offers dementia care at home with trained professionals who provide gentle, patient-centered care.

## Approach

Care plans are tailored to daily needs, habits and stage of memory loss. Care at home helps reduce confusion and maintain a calming daily routine. Caregivers use calming techniques and simple routines to create a secure environment.

### What is included in dementia care?

Personalized memory support with familiar routines and gentle reminders, medication and safety supervision, and emotional companionship, all delivered at home.

### Are caregivers trained in dementia care?

The page states that caregivers are trained in dementia-specific care techniques. It does not name training programs, certifications or hours.

### Is dementia care available part-time or only full-time?

Plans are personalized and range from part-time support to full-time live-in care.

### Can dementia care reduce confusion and anxiety?

The page says calming techniques and consistent routines are used for this purpose.
