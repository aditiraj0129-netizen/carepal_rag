# Lisa, the voice assistant in The CarePal app

Lisa is described as your voice assistant for care at home. It is powered by Google Gemini through The CarePal's secure service and is available on Android and iPhone.

## What Lisa can do

Lisa can check today's medicines and whether they were marked as taken, find the assigned nurse and their attendance, prepare a nurse booking form, look up upcoming appointments and start a doctor request, draft and send a support ticket, and open app screens such as documents.

### Does Lisa give medical advice?

No. Lisa does not give medical advice and is not for emergencies. Users are told to contact a doctor or nurse, or their local emergency number.

### Is my data safe with Lisa?

Lisa is off until the user agrees to share their voice and records. Bookings are drafts and nothing is booked or paid until the user confirms. Voice recordings are not kept. Members can try two free conversations of up to four minutes each.
