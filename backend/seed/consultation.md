# Consultation

Professional medical consultations and expert healthcare advice online or at home, to help people decide whether a situation needs urgent care.

## Types of consultation

- Doctors give medical advice, help with treatment plans and support early check-ins.
- Nurses handle tasks such as wound care, injections, vitals monitoring and post-surgery support.
- Therapists offer mental health support for grief, parenting, aging, relationships and caregiver burnout.

### How do I book a consultation?

The Book Consultation button leads to the contact page. You can also use Talk to an Expert, Book an Appointment, or the WhatsApp chat link. No prices are stated.
