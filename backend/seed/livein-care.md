# Live-in Care

Comprehensive 24/7 care in the comfort of your home, with no move to a facility needed. A caregiver is always present, offering peace of mind day and night.

## What live-in care covers

- Daily routines such as bathing, dressing and personal hygiene.
- Companionship, conversation and shared meals.
- Medication reminders, so medications are taken on time as prescribed by your doctor.
- Meal preparation adapted to dietary needs and preferences.
- Mobility assistance with walking and walking aids.
- Regular updates to the family about how the person is doing.

### How is a caregiver matched for live-in care?
