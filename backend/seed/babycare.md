# Baby Care

Newborn and infant care at home for new parents, with trained caregivers who focus on warmth, patience and attention to safety.

## What baby care includes

Caregivers help with feeding, bathing, soothing and diapering, and follow the parents' preferred routines.

### How do I book baby care?

Use the Book Baby Care button, or schedule a free consultation first. The page says no commitment is required.

### Is there a free consultation?

Yes, the page offers a free consultation before booking.

### Are caregivers available outside scheduled shifts?

The page says yes, for busy or tiring periods. The page does not state shift lengths, minimum bookings, postnatal-specific services or prices.
