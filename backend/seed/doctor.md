# Doctor Consultation

TheCarePal connects users with doctors for on-call or scheduled consultations, without waiting rooms.

## Consultation options

You can choose between video, phone or in-home consultations. Doctors include general physicians, pediatricians and geriatric experts, and more. Doctors assess your condition, ask the right questions and help you understand what is going on.

### Can I get a prescription?

Medications or therapy suggestions are provided only when truly necessary, based on your condition.

### Are chronic condition check-ins supported?

Yes. Regular check-ins for conditions such as blood pressure and diabetes are offered through home and phone consultations.
