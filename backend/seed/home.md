# The CarePal

Trusted caretakers and home nurses for elderly parents in India. Smarter health starts with CarePal.

## Services

Care solutions: Live-in Care (24/7), Alzheimer Care, Dementia Care, Elderly Care, Autism Care, Baby Care and Emergency Care. Consultations: Doctor Consultation, Nurse Consultation and Mental Therapy.

## How booking works in the app

Open the CarePal app, go to Services and submit a care request. The app matches you with a carer and shares their details. Then complete subscription details, review the bill summary and confirm on the payment page.

### Is there an app?

Yes, for iOS and Android. Login options are available for users, nurses and employees.
