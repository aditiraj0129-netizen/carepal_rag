# Mental Therapy

One-on-one sessions with licensed professionals, accessible through the CarePal app from home.

### What kind of therapy is offered?

Sessions cover everyday emotional concerns such as stress, anxiety, loneliness and emotional well-being, and are not just crisis support.

### Is therapy only for older adults?

No. Therapy is offered at every life stage, from young adults to seniors.

### How do I book therapy?

Use the Get The CarePal's Therapy button or sign up to be matched with a licensed therapist. Nurse consultation is listed as professional nursing advice from the comfort of your home. No prices are stated.
