# Services

TheCarePal offers home care and health services across India.

## Featured services

- Baby Care: from newborn care to toddler support.
- Elderly Care: experienced caregivers for companionship and assistance at home.
- Skilled Nursing Support: connects users with licensed nurses for medication management, post-surgery care and ongoing health monitoring.
- Personalized Home Health Services: physiotherapy, wound care and wellness checks delivered at home.

## Care solutions

Live-in Care (24/7), Alzheimer Care, Dementia Care, Elderly Care, Autism Care, Baby Care and Emergency Care. Consultations: Doctor Consultation, Nurse Consultation and Mental Therapy.
