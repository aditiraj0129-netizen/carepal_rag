# Autism Care

Structured, sensory-aware care at home for children and adults, with routines tailored to sensory needs and emotional balance.

## What autism care includes

- Personalized daily routines and sensory-friendly approaches.
- Emotional and social support and gentle guidance for communication.
- Help with learning and development such as communication, motor skills and everyday tasks.
- Family collaboration with regular updates.

### Is autism care available for adults?

Yes. Support covers both children and adults and is tailored to different ages and stages.
