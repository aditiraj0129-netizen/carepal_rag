# Alzheimer's Care

Expert Alzheimer's disease care and specialized memory support delivered at home by trained caregivers. Alzheimer's is described as a progressive neurological disorder affecting memory, thinking and behavior, and the most common cause of dementia.

## Services

- Daily routine assistance: help with bathing, dressing, eating and medication, built around familiarity and comfort.
- Safety and supervision: a secure, structured environment to prevent wandering, confusion or falls.
- Memory support activities: simple, engaging tasks to stimulate the mind.
- Behavioral and mood support: responding to anxiety, restlessness or aggression with empathy and patience.
- Family support and updates: keeping families informed and involved.

### How is Alzheimer's care different from general elderly care?

It focuses on disease-specific memory and cognitive decline.

### Which stages of Alzheimer's are supported?

Caregivers support families through the full progression, from early memory loss to advanced stages.
