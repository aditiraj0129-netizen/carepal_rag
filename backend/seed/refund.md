# Refund Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2025.

## Policy

All payments made for our services are non-refundable. Once a purchase is complete, no refunds, cancellations, or reimbursements will be issued, regardless of usage or service satisfaction.

## Questions or concerns

The policy does not list exceptions, deadlines or a process for requesting a refund. For questions or concerns, contact the team through thecarepal.com or by email at support@thecarepal.com.
