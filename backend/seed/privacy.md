# Privacy Policy

The CarePal is operated by Carepalz Internet Private Limited. The policy is effective April 10, 2025 and states it was last updated on October 15, 2026.

## Information collected

The website collects personal data such as email address, first and last name, phone number and address details, plus usage data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and device identifiers. The iOS app also handles health data entered by the user or recorded by nurses (steps, stand hours, height, weight and other vitals), booking details, sensitive medical information such as blood group and chronic conditions, and support tickets with text and images that are reviewed manually.

## Do you sell my personal information?

No. The policy states: We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third-party. Data is shared with certified nurses during bookings and with service providers such as Google Firebase, and with Google Gemini for AI features only after the user gives permission.

## How long is my data kept?

Data is kept only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes and for legal needs. Data sent for optional AI features is deleted no longer than 30 days from submission.

### How do I delete or correct my data?

Users may request access, correction and deletion of their data. Deletion is requested through support at support@thecarepal.com. AI permissions can be reviewed or withdrawn in the app under Profile, Utility, AI Privacy. Withdrawing AI permission does not delete data that was already sent or saved.

## Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase services with encryption and infrastructure-level security. Role-based access control applies and all access is logged. The policy acknowledges that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## Children

The app is not intended for users under the age of 13.

## Contact

Email support@thecarepal.com or phone +91 84358 96270. The policy does not name a separate grievance officer.
