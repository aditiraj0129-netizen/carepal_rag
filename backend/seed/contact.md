# Contact

## How to reach the team

You can message TheCarePal on WhatsApp at +91 78558 16586, use the contact form, or schedule a consultation through the scheduling widget on the page. The contact form asks for first name, last name, email address, phone number, subject and message.

## Support levels stated on the page

The page shows 24/7 Support and under 60 minutes response as headline figures, without explaining what they measure.
